# Phase 7 MV — The Movement Genome (preliminary design)

v1.0 · 2026-08-30 · Decisions D1–D7 **resolved** (owner, 2026-08-30) — recorded in §5. Built on docs/movement-genome-research.md (the four-lens research and its §6 synthesis) and the substrate inventory in that document's §1. Owner order context: the UI block (species panel, locus visual grammar) remains queued ahead of this phase, but per D7 nothing in this phase depends on it.

## 1. Principles (carried in from the research, restated as build rules)

1. **Parameters, never controllers.** Wiring, taxis rung, arbitration ordering, and draw structure stay species identity. Loci are scalar, monotone, one-signed, expressed draw-neutrally at the sites inventoried in research §1.2, exactly neutral at g0.
2. **Price realized kinematics.** Motion loci ride the existing quadratic-in-realized-velocity cost; navigation loci (set-points, gains) are priced by exposure through the fields, not by upkeep. New harness assertion at every movement corridor rail: energy per realized distance stays monotone (the anti-Sims check).
3. **Certify in the loop.** Movement corridors are certified across field layouts (shipped sun, hot sun, heater, press, unwarmed), not only genome corners — a movement locus balanced under one geometry is not thereby balanced under another (H.3 corner extinctions are the precedent).
4. **Instrument before knob.** MV.0 ships the movement observatory and measures reference bands in the shipped world *before* any locus goes live.
5. **Legibility or explicit exemption.** Every locus passes the 10-second rail test or is designated Observatory-only in its record. Per D7 the test applies to *motion itself* — path shape, trails, aggregation — not to a body-form channel; movement-strategy loci carry no visual-grammar encoding.
6. **One structural rule amendment is required** (CONTRIBUTING/genetics-scaling): today "a locus expression may only scale a rate or a probability, never a stock." A set-point locus *shifts a reference*. Proposed amendment: "…scale a rate or a probability **or shift a bounded reference/threshold**, never a stock." `checkLocus` gains a case bounding additive reference spans the way it bounds multipliers. This is an owner-visible rule change (D1 gates the phase; the amendment ships with MV.1).

- Name: "Restlessness — exploration vs exploitation." Syndrome expression with fixed signs at the two existing drift draws: noise × (1 + rsSlope·d) and damp pulled toward 1 by a bounded term (rover = stronger kicks, straighter persistence; sitter = the reverse). ## 4. What this phase does *not* claim

The flagship's expected first result at shipped σ is that evolution does **not** outrun the trap — the phase's honest deliverable is a world that can finally be *asked*, plus the measured escape threshold if one exists in the legal σ range. Inherited set-points are recorded as the model's known departure from biology (which learns them — research §3.4) in this plan, before measurement, the way the Yoshida non-reproduction was recorded.

## 9. Instrument incident — corridor fuzz ran at ×1 from Phase 6 to MV.1 (found 2026-08-30, fixed)

corridor.js multiplied every σ×4 *before* its seed loop; the Phase 6 `LOCUS_SHIPPED` restore in initWorld resets σ on every start, so each fuzz run actually executed at shipped σ. Caught by MV.1's own battery: the "fuzz" seed-77 collapse reproduced tune2's shipped-σ collapse tick-exact (t=12,851, identical populations) — only an unmultiplied σ explains a bit-identical trajectory. Consequence for the records: every "fuzz 8/8" claim since Phase 6 (H.3, H.4, H.5 stabilization passes) was a *shipped-σ, 54k-tick* long-horizon check — still meaningful, but not the ×4 adversary it was labeled as; the rails and corners are unaffected (mutation off). Fixed by setting σ after start, per seed; the true ×4 fuzz re-run on the MV.1 build is part of this phase's record. The meta-lesson is the project's own: the instrument is only as honest as the calibration fights behind it — and a fingerprint-identical reproduction (tune2 vs fuzz) is what caught it.

## 10. Forward plan — Cilio advanced movement (MV-C, drafted 2026-08-30; enters after MV.3/MV.4)

The grazer is the world's only species that is both hunter and hunted, so its movement loci price themselves through two roles at once — no synthetic cost needed anywhere below. All candidates are draw-neutral at the steer branch's existing sites (research §1.2); ranked by anchor strength:

**C1 — Boldness (flee threshold; the lead candidate).** `flee.sense` (0.06) scaled by a locus: bold lines ignore more alarm scent, forage through danger, and die more to Venator; shy lines flee early and eat less. The literature's cleanest diversifying candidate — flight-initiation economics (Ydenberg & Dill) plus heritable boldness under *fluctuating* selection (Dingemanse's great tits: bold and shy each win in different years) predicts the diversifying detector, not a sweep, and Venator's own knife-edged presence/absence supplies the fluctuation for free. Expression: multiplicative on flee.sense at the draw-free alarm comparison; caution from research §4.7 — the flee *ordering* stays fixed, only the threshold moves, and no draw may become conditional. Entry measurement: flee.sense surface sweep (the --surface pattern) to size the span; watch the alarmEmit feedback (a bold-majority swarm emits less usable alarm — a possible frequency dependence, the honest test being invasion-from-rare).

**C2 — Post-capture strategy locus: kill-and-stay ↔ kill-and-move (owner concept, 2026-08-30; supersedes the plain giving-up threshold).** A two-phase program after every successful feed — AFTERGLOW (stay close: speed down, turning up; where one Drifta was, the bloom is) then RELOCATE (move on: speed up, straight) — encoded as **a fixed state machine with heritable parameters, never evolvable program structure** (the steps are species identity like the taxis rung; only the dials evolve — the Venator burst state machine on `W.bst` is the in-codebase precedent, and evolvable step-structure is the recorded rejected alternative: illegible and draw-count-unsafe). Shipped with the strictest guarantee: at g0 every phase multiplier is exactly 1.0 — the timers tick, nothing expresses, the world is bit-identical; genotype deviation turns the program on (the warmth-gating pattern generalized to a behaviour gate). One axis first: g high = stayer (long, tight afterglow), g low = mover (strong relocation); a later duration-vs-intensity second locus only if the strategy space earns it. Prices already live in the world: stayers deplete the clump they sit on AND linger where Venator hunts; movers pay quadratic speed cost and leave food behind. **This locus inherits the phase's frequency-dependence question** from MV.2's null: stayers-common favours movers and vice versa — patch depletion through the consumer, where the rover/sitter mechanism actually lives. Storage note: needs one per-organism phase timer (a new W array — a storage change, initialized in spawn, draw-free) — flagged for the increment's conformance note.
