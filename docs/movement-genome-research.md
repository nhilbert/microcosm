# MICROCOSM — Movement Genome Research

v1.0 · 2026-08-30 · Four lenses (biology · molecular biology · cybernetics · gaming/alife) over the measured substrate, then the synthesis the design phase will build on. Sources searched 2026-08-30. The two strongest empirical anchors — Fraebel's evolved E. coli swimmers and the *Drosophila* rover/sitter polymorphism — are, like Lenski and Yoshida were for the genetics phase, the best evidence available anywhere for what evolution does to movement in exactly our kind of system.

## 1. The substrate — what movement is today, and where a locus may legally express

**1.1 Three controllers, all parameters species constants.** Movement dispatches on `T.movement`: `drift` (Drifta — damped random walk, two unconditional draws per tick, plus draw-free phototaxis and thermotaxis vector terms), `tumble` (Bacillus, Necro — klinokinesis: one draw against the tumble threshold, a second for the new heading only if it fires; the thermal discomfort penalty already modifies the comparand draw-free), `steer` (Cilio, Venator — three mutually exclusive sub-branches: flee 0 draws, pursue 0–2 draws only at contact via the escape jink, idle exactly 1 draw), and sessile (Solara — zero draws, zero cost). Not one movement parameter is locus-modulated today: `LOCUS_DEFAULTS` carries no speed, noise, damp, thermo, or tumble slope. The two shipped loci that touch movement-adjacent code (escSlope, catchSlope) express at the existing `R()<escP` contact draw — the established pattern.

**1.2 The legal expression sites are already mapped.** The RNG-order contract permits, draw-neutrally: in `drift`, any modulation of damp, noise, phototaxis, driftSpeed, moveCostMul, thermo gain, or the set-point compared at the thermotaxis branch (the H.2/H.3 vector terms are the precedent for fully draw-free steering modifications); in `tumble`, tumbleLow/tumbleHigh (they only set the threshold of an existing unconditional draw — the thermal klinokinesis already does exactly this), speed, and the comparand; in `steer`, speed, turnRate, sense, satiation, torpor, flee.*, burst.*, the idle-turn amplitude at its existing draw, and the idle thermo set-point. Illegal without a declared ecology change: any new `R()` on a shared path, and any genotype-conditional skipping of an existing draw. Free slots at MAXLOCI 4: Drifta 2, Cilio 3, Bacillus 2, Venator 4 (locus-naive today). The *real* ceiling is the Observatory: the recorder carries two locus planes per species (channels 42–49, 75–82; detector arrays sized 7×2) — a third locus on Drifta or Bacillus is invisible without a declared recorder extension and rebaseline.

**1.3 The standing owner mandate.** The thermal trap is the movement genome's founding problem, on record three times: Drifta's set-point (`tpref = topt = +9`, gain 0.6) makes a +8 core pure attraction and loses the world 8/8, attributed by A/B; the H.5 thermal locus did not rescue it (collapse outruns selection at σ 0.03); the owner's closing decision — "the sun stays lethal… the set-point itself remains movement-genome territory, where evolution — not a reprice — may one day discover a better controller." Both hunters were deliberately blinded (`thermo: 0`) after a fed Cilio's set-point walked it away from its prey (core lost 3/8): "the hunters stay blind until the movement genome can price a set-point for them." And the visual channel is pre-reserved: body form elongated↔circular = speed/mobility loci (owner decision 2026-08-30), colliding with shape-as-species-identity — the movement locus and the grammar increment must ship sequenced together.

## 2. Biology — what actually evolves when movement is under selection

**2.1 The framework (Nathan et al. 2008, PNAS).** Every path is the joint output of internal state × motion capacity × navigation capacity × external factors. Microcosm implements all four boxes already (energy gates; speed/turn kernels; taxis gains and set-points; fields). The framework's design-relevant claim: put loci in *distinct boxes with distinct prices* — motion capacity is priced in energy per tick, navigation capacity is nearly free metabolically and priced in *exposure*, and navigation is where the largest fitness consequences live. That is a precise description of the thermal trap: a free parameter with lethal consequences.

**2.2 Movement parameters evolve fast, along trade-offs (the Fraebel anchor).** Fraebel et al. 2017 (eLife): E. coli selected for migration through soft agar doubled migration rate in ~5 rounds via heritable changes in run speed, run duration, and tumble frequency — and evolved *along* a run-speed↔growth-rate trade-off whose position depended on the medium. Van Ditmarsch et al. 2013: 20/20 independent swarming-selected P. aeruginosa lines converged on hyperswarming point mutations at the price of biofilm formation. The flagellum costs ~5–10% of the total budget (Schavemaker & Lynch 2022) and motility is lost fast and repeatedly when useless. Prescriptions: tumble/run statistics are legitimately monogenic-scale heritable; price them against division; expect local adaptation within tens of generations and motility *collapse* where food is uniform — a spatially structured polymorphism for free.

**2.3 Lévy walks: use the pattern, not the mechanism.** The Lévy foraging hypothesis (Viswanathan 1999; Sims/Humphries 2008–2012: sparse prey → heavy-tailed superdiffusive search, dense prey → Brownian) is real as phenomenology but contested as mechanism — the original albatross result was overturned (Edwards 2007), the one heritability claim (mussels, de Jager 2011) refuted on re-analysis, and composite intensive/extensive switching fits better and forages better (Benhamou 2007; Bénichou 2011). Do not encode a Lévy exponent. Encode the **intensive↔extensive balance** — turn-angle variance, straightness, encounter-triggered slowdown. The heritable anchor for that axis is rover/sitter (§3.3): straight alleles win sparse/depleted worlds, tortuous alleles win dense patches, and negative frequency dependence can hold both.

**2.4 Thermal preference, the Bogert effect, and traps — the trap literature is our literature.** Measured Tpref heritability is *low* (h² ≈ 0.066 in D. subobscura, Castañeda 2019) and genetically uncorrelated with tolerance — preference and tolerance evolve independently, which retroactively explains why the H.5 tolerance locus couldn't answer a preference problem. The Bogert effect (Huey, Hertz & Sinervo 2003): behavioral thermoregulation shields physiology from selection — an evolved set-point that walks Drifta out of the warm core should measurably *stall* selection on the shipped thermal-compensation locus, a two-locus interaction our per-locus channels can already see. And the evolutionary-trap literature (Schlaepfer 2002; Robertson 2013) names our situation exactly: a trap is escapable only by evolving the cue-response itself, only if genetic variance exists in the preference, and only if mortality doesn't outrun selection — trap escape should be threshold-like in mutation supply and severity. All three predictions are harness-testable.

**2.5 Dispersal never evolves to zero, and evolution can happen through space.** Hamilton & May 1977: kin competition alone maintains substantial dispersal even in stable saturated habitats. Zera & Denno 1997: the wing-dimorphic crickets price mobility against early fecundity — the best-measured allocation trade-off in physiology. Cane toads (Phillips 2006; Shine 2011): **spatial sorting** — the fastest dispersers meet at the invasion front and breed there, assembling dispersal phenotypes through space rather than fitness, the "Olympic village effect". For us: after any seeding or collapse-recolonization, high-mobility alleles should transiently enrich at expanding edges *without any pricing at all* — an emergent, narratable signature (allele frequency vs. distance from the population core).

**2.6 Predator–prey movement: out-turn beats out-run.** Howland 1974: escape success depends on speed *and* turning-radius ratios; a slower, tighter-turning prey escapes a faster predator. Wilson et al. 2018 (Nature; cheetah–impala collars): predators are ~38% faster yet prey maximize survival by running *below* maximum speed, preserving manoeuvre. Flight initiation economics (Ydenberg & Dill 1986) and heritable, fluctuating-selection-maintained boldness (Dingemanse 2002–04, great tits: selection oscillates with beech mast, maintaining variance) make a flee-threshold locus a natural *diversifying* candidate rather than a sweep. Protean escape (Domenici 2011; heritable escape unpredictability, JEB 2025) is a later extension of the existing jink.

**2.7 Speed's price must be superlinear.** Schmidt-Nielsen 1972 cost-of-transport plus Ware 1978 optimal foraging speed: drag makes power rise as v²–v³, encounter gain rises only linearly, so the optimal speed is interior and shifts with food density. A linear price predicts a runaway rail; a quadratic price predicts fast alleles in rich patches and slow alleles in poor ones from one mechanism. Our drift and steer branches already charge quadratic-in-realized-velocity; **the tumble branch does not** — it charges `T.speed²` flat, whether or not the organism moved that way, a pre-existing inconsistency a Bacillus movement locus would sit right on top of (flagged for the design phase, not silently fixed: it is step()-reachable arithmetic).

## 3. Molecular biology — which parameters real genomes actually hold

**3.1 The E. coli che circuit: tumble frequency is the most literal genotype→controller dial in biology.** Receptors→CheA→CheY-P sets clockwise (tumble) probability; CheR/CheB methylation feedback is the adaptation integrator (Berg & Brown 1972; Barkai & Leibler 1998). Single knockouts give clean discrete controllers (cheR⁻ smooth swimmer, cheB⁻/cheZ⁻ incessant tumbler), and the *natural* axis is quantitative: persistent clonal individuality in tumble bias, itself selectable (Spudich & Koshland 1976; eLife 2014). A Bacillus locus scaling the tumble thresholds is not a metaphor — it is the che circuit's job description. Notably, even E. coli's "set-point" is a within-lifetime integrator: the methylation state stores the recent background.

**3.2 One residue can flip a whole foraging strategy — but it flips a syndrome, not a parameter.** C. elegans npr-1: one amino acid separates social (aggregating, fast) from solitary (dispersing, slow) wild strains, moving locomotion speed, O₂ preference, and aggregation together (de Bono & Bargmann 1998). Roaming↔dwelling is set by opposing neuromodulators (serotonin vs. PDF, Flavell 2013), and monoamines generally are broadcast gain knobs that co-vary speed, turning, and persistence — which is *why* boldness syndromes exist. The architecture lesson: biology's movement loci are mostly **syndrome loci** — one locus, several movement parameters, fixed signs — and independent one-parameter loci are the exception. Fewer loci with honest pleiotropy also makes better stories.

**3.3 Rover/sitter — the canonical balanced movement polymorphism, and our best shippable anchor.** *Drosophila* `for` (PKG; Osborne 1997, Science): rovers travel far while feeding, sitters exploit the current patch; ~70:30 in nature; selected apart by larval density in three independent base populations (Sokolowski 1997, PNAS) and maintained by demonstrated **negative frequency-dependent selection** — each morph wins when rare (Fitzpatrick 2007, Nature). Every shipped Microcosm locus is balanced by a *price*; rover/sitter is balanced by *frequency*, a genuinely new maintenance mechanism the harness can test directly (invasion-from-rare, both directions). The same molecule (PKG/egl-4) tunes exploration in worms — this axis is deeply conserved.

**3.4 Thermal set-points: genes hold the machinery and gain; experience often holds the target.** Drosophila TrpA1 is the molecular thermometer; variants and isoforms shift preferred temperature by ~1 °C, populations differ by a few °C along clines riding chromosomal inversions (Hamada 2008; Kang 2012; Rego/Rand 2010). But the sharpest single fact in this lens is the C. elegans AFD result: the worm's thermotaxis set-point is its *cultivation temperature* — learned in hours, not inherited; genes set whether the memory circuit works and how fast it updates. **A purely inherited set-point is a declared simplification**: biology mostly inherits gains, thresholds, and transition rates, and *learns* targets — plasticity is biology's trap-escape mechanism, which is precisely why our hard-coded set-point produced a trap. The honest middle path, if ever wanted as its own declared change: an inherited *relaxation rate* toward locally experienced conditions — then the rate is the locus, exactly how thermotaxis-plasticity genes work. For now: small set-point shifts (a fraction of niche width), slow σ, and the departure recorded.

**3.5 Architecture and effect sizes.** Within-population activity variation is polygenic, h² ≈ 0.2–0.5 (Garland's High Runner mice: ~61 small regions, four replicate lines taking different genomic routes — yet one large-effect Mendelian passenger, mini-muscle, swept in one line). Between strategies, single large-effect loci are real: blackcap migration direction is additive-heritable and re-routed itself in the wild in ~30 years (Berthold 1992, Nature); salmon vgll3 explains ~39% of a movement-adjacent life-history decision. With MAXLOCI 4 we cannot and should not simulate polygenicity — each locus is a *large-effect strategy allele* (the between-population regime, which single loci demonstrably occupy) and mutation σ plays the polygenic background. Also expect and narrate **loss**: monarchs bred without migration lose it; flagella decay in a flask; movement alleles sliding to the cheap rail whenever the world stops paying for them is one of the best-documented outcomes in this whole literature — the Observatory should treat it as a story, not a failure.

## 4. Cybernetics — the controller lens

**4.1 A movement genome is a vector of signed gains (Braitenberg 1984).** The entire vehicle repertoire {fear, aggression, love, explore} is spanned by two bits of wiring (crossed?, sign?) plus scalar gains. Prescription and warning in one: loci should be gains on existing sensor→motor couplings, wiring topology stays species identity — and a gain crossing zero mid-corridor is a *qualitative* behavior flip (fear→aggression), a discontinuous ecology inside one locus. Either keep movement loci one-signed (magnitude heritable, sign fixed) or treat a zero-crossing as a deliberate, narrated regime boundary. Non-monotone transfer (Vehicle 4) is the `curve` machinery — already a slider, keep it out of the shipped loci.

**4.2 Grey Walter's moth lesson.** M. speculatrix (1949) got lifelike exploration from one photocell and two tubes — our drift branch *is* the tortoise — but Walter had to build in the aversion limb: attraction to moderate light, aversion to bright, so the tortoise orbits instead of fixating. A monotone attraction with heritable gain selects toward source-fixation and death at a lethal source. We measured that: 8/8. The genome must include either the aversion limb (a set-point, not a gain) or a price that makes fixation expensive.

**4.3 Ashby's ultrastability is the architecture we already chose.** The Homeostat's double loop — fast feedback holding essential variables, and a slow loop making random step-changes to the fast loop's *parameters* when an essential variable escapes — is, in Ashby's own reading, evolution with death as the bound and mutation as the step. A per-lifetime-fixed set-point mutated only at division is ultrastability implemented deterministically with zero extra draws. Its documented failure mode is also ours, already measured: when death is fast relative to the slow loop, ultrastability fails *by design* ("evolution does not outrun the thermal trap", 8/8) — the Observatory should narrate that honestly rather than the genome being tuned to hide it.

**4.4 Behavior lives in the loop (Beer 1995), so certify in the loop.** A movement locus's phenotype is not "taxis gain 0.7"; it is "packs 20% tighter around the sun, apex holds 4/8" — which is literally how H.3 was accepted. Consequence for the ritual: a movement locus balanced under one field geometry can be unbalanced under another, so **corridor certification must sample field layouts** (suns, heaters, presses), not only genome corners. The H.3 extreme-corner extinctions were this lesson's first installment.

**4.5 The taxis ladder stays fixed; the rungs' gains evolve (Fraenkel & Gunn 1940).** Orthokinesis, klinokinesis, klinotaxis, tropotaxis, telotaxis — each rung buys straighter approach for more sensory machinery, and our three controllers sit on distinct rungs. The rung a species runs is structural (a controller switch changes draw counts — contract-illegal as mere expression); the clean heritable quantities are per-rung gains and thresholds. One specific caution: a heritable klinokinesis *memory length* has a non-monotone fitness surface (too short = no signal, too long = inverted bias) — prefer tumble-gain loci, avoid memory-length loci.

**4.6 Evolutionary robotics: few, smooth, monotone — and mind our own reality gap.** Nolfi & Floreano's consolidated lessons: low-dimensional genotypes with smooth monotone genotype→behavior maps evolve reliably; rugged or deceptive landscapes stall; honest aggregate fitness (survival/reproduction — which is all our selection is) beats shaped fitness. Two transfers: epistasis between movement loci acts *through position* (taxis gain × speed interact even when each is monotone — certify the joint corridor), and the sim has its own reality gap — selection will find field-discretization seams (cell boundaries, wrap edges) if any exist to park on.

**4.7 Arbitration: ordering fixed, thresholds heritable (Brooks 1986; Ijspeert 2008).** Our implicit subsumption stack (flee > pursue/feed > taxis > wander) should keep its *ordering* as species identity — fixed priority is what stays analyzable — while the *thresholds* between layers (flee trigger, hunger override, satiation) are scalar, monotone, and exactly what CPG biology says evolution retunes. A threshold locus must never change *whether* a draw happens — the pre-draw pattern (draw unconditionally, gate the use) is mandatory where a threshold sits upstream of a draw.

**4.8 Perceptual control theory names the flagship (Powers 1973).** Organisms vary action to keep *perceived* variables at internal reference values; the invariant across environments is the reference, so the natural evolvable quantity is the reference — preferred warmth, preferred light — with loop gains secondary. PCT's pathology is our measured incident: an evolutionary trap is **a correct controller with a stale reference** — the loop works perfectly and marches the population to its death. The set-point locus is therefore not one candidate among many; it is the movement genome's reason for existing, per the owner's own closing decision.

**4.9 What the Observatory should measure (all pure observers, zero draws).** Mean squared displacement vs. lag (diffusive/ballistic/confined — the single best controller discriminator; a set-point sweep shows first as confinement radius shrinking around the preferred isotherm); occupancy entropy per species (requisite variety made measurable — falling entropy = aggregation or a trap forming); gradient alignment (mean cos θ between velocity and each field gradient — the direct readout of realized taxis, separating "gene changed" from "field changed"); straightness/mean run length (the tumble and wander loci's first signature); and the trap detector — the joint statistic of occupancy vs. per-cell realized energy balance, narrated per rule 6 as "since": *the population is concentrating where its budget is negative*. Per-genotype-quartile versions give movement loci their selection-story channel: alignment diverging between g-quartiles is visible before allele frequencies certify a sweep.

## 5. Gaming and alife — legibility, prices, and the exploit catalog

**5.1 The scalar-vs-network verdict is binary across ~10 systems.** Primer's simulations (heritable speed/size/sense with a posted price, energy ∝ size³·speed² + sense) let millions of viewers narrate selection unaided; Sebastian Lague's ecosystem does the same with trait-to-visual mapping. Every evolved-network system — Bibites brains, Polyworld, ALIEN, NERO, MarI/O — produces behavior its own creators must reverse-engineer offline. The structural reason: a network has no stable coordinates — no axis for a mean, a band, a corridor, or a "sweep vs diversifying" verdict, and topology mutation breaks any fixed-draw contract. **Neuroevolution is the design's principal rejected alternative, rejected for four reasons: no bands, no detectors, no draw-count contract, no nameable stories.** Scalar loci with declared prices are not the modest option; they are the only option compatible with what this project *is*.

**5.2 Reynolds gives the parameter vocabulary humans can see.** Forty years of steering behaviors (boids 1987; GDC 1999) codified the knobs that read as alive: max speed, turn authority (max_force — the force/speed *ratio* is what registers as fish vs. insect), wander persistence/amplitude/decorrelation (the projected-circle factorization maps cleanly onto our damp/noise pair), pursuit lead, arrive/flee radii. Known dead knobs: blend weights between competing behaviors — invisible until they flip discontinuously. Our drift branch is already an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck wander; a rover/sitter locus is a persistence knob on machinery Reynolds already taught players to read.

**5.3 The exploit catalog (Sims 1994; Lehman et al. 2018; the Bibites).** Evolution optimizes the simulator, not the intent: Sims' creatures twitched to harvest integrator error; the Lehman anthology catalogs dozens more. The Bibites' community catalog is *dominated* by movement pathologies — spinners (constant rotation as free scanning, once vision is anisotropic and rotation cheap), wall-huggers, spiral searchers — and its dev's fixes were: price rotation, and build population-statistics tooling because individuals were unreadable. Thrive prices flagella as ATP line-items and still fights the degenerate sessile attractor (if not-moving out-earns moving, evolution abandons motion). Standing prescriptions: **price realized kinematics, never requested** (a speed allele riding the driftSpeed clamp must not get distance cheaper than the formula assumes); assert energy-per-realized-distance stays monotone across every movement corridor at the rails (the anti-Sims harness check); watch the slow rail for the sessile attractor and the fast rail for clamp-riding; check whether any of our gradient reads reward oscillatory heading.

**5.4 The Creatures test.** Creatures (1996) made a 952-neuron brain lovable by *instruments*: the Science Kit graphed drives, and norns externalized state in posture and speech. The transferable rule: every movement locus ships with one plotted vital and one visible motion correlate — and at our sprite sizes (4 px on a phone), the perceivable channels are path shape (straightness/tortuosity), speed with body elongation as the redundant static cue (the reserved grammar channel), short motion trails, and turn crispness. **The 10-second test: two populations at opposite rails, side by side — if a naive viewer cannot tell which is which in 10 seconds, the locus is carried by the Observatory alone or it is cut.** Spore's deeper contract applies to the grammar increment: form must predict motion and motion must confirm form, or one of them reads as a lie.

**5.5 Determinism at the port boundary.** Lockstep doctrine (Factorio, Gaffer on Games): same-binary float determinism is achievable; cross-platform is where transcendentals bite. Movement kernels are exactly where `sin/cos/atan2` live in step()-reachable code — docs/porting.md should mandate bit-reproducible implementations (polynomial or table) for any transcendental a Kotlin port must match, and the movement genome adds none of its own if loci only modulate magnitudes consumed by unconditionally executed draws — which the RNG contract already requires.

## 6. Synthesis — what the design phase should build (research conclusions, pre-design)

**6.1 The genome is controller *parameters*, never controllers.** Wiring, taxis rung, arbitration ordering, and draw structure stay species identity; loci are scalar, monotone, one-signed magnitudes expressed at the existing draw-neutral sites (§1.2) through the existing factor machinery (`1 + slope·d − curve·d²`, curve 0). This is simultaneously the RNG contract's demand, evolutionary robotics' evolvability result, biology's actual architecture (gains and thresholds, §3.4), and the alife legibility verdict. Neuroevolution, Lévy-exponent loci, controller-switching loci, and memory-length loci are the recorded rejected alternatives (§5.1, §2.3, §4.5).

**6.2 Candidate loci, ranked by anchor strength and mandate.**
1. **Drifta thermal set-point** (`tpref` as g0 ± a small offset, a fraction of niche width; expression at the existing draw-free thermotaxis comparison) — the flagship, mandated by the owner's trap decision, architected by Ashby/PCT (§4.3, §4.8), anchored by TrpA1 clines (§3.4). Priced by the field itself: the set-point *is* exposure. Small σ is biologically honest (Tpref h² ≈ 0.07).
2. **Drifta rover/sitter** (persistence: damp/noise or an intensive↔extensive balance at the two existing drift draws) — the strongest balanced-polymorphism anchor in the entire literature (§3.3), a *frequency*-balanced locus where all six shipped loci are *price*-balanced: a genuinely new maintenance mechanism, and the doubly-heritable Drifta patch story (defense already separates by patch) gains a dispersal axis.
3. **Bacillus tumble locus** (tumbleLow/High thresholds at the existing draw) — the most literal genotype→controller mapping in biology (§3.1), priced Fraebel-style against division; predicted outcome: motility investment collapsing where detritus is uniform — spatial polymorphism and narratable *loss* (§3.5).
4. **Hunter set-point loci** (Cilio/Venator `thermo` gain from 0 with a heritable set-point, idle branch, draw-free) — the second half of the owner mandate: evolution pricing what hard-coding got wrong (3/8 core loss). A hunter set-point is only viable as a locus *because* selection can move it; note Venator at N≈25 is drift-dominated — its locus would random-walk, and the genetics-phase honesty rule (no adaptation headlines for the apex) extends here unchanged.
5. **Speed/flee-threshold loci** (later): driftSpeed/steer speed on the quadratic price with the reserved elongation channel; a Cilio/Drifta boldness threshold as the diversifying-detector candidate (§2.6, §4.7). Deferred behind 1–3 by scope rule: one extension, then defer.

**6.3 Prices, by structure not theory.** Motion loci: superlinear (quadratic) in *realized* velocity — already true in drift/steer; the tumble branch's flat `T.speed²` charge is a pre-existing inconsistency the design phase must decide on *before* a Bacillus speed-adjacent locus ships (a declared change if touched). A small fixed machinery cost is what makes movement *losable* (§2.2, §3.5) — worth considering where it does not already exist. Navigation loci (set-points, gains): priced by exposure through the existing Q10/light budgets, not by upkeep — per Nathan (§2.1), that near-free-but-consequential structure is the point, and it is what makes them trap-capable, which is their scientific interest. Instrument before knob stands: measure each price surface before choosing slopes, as 5.7 did.

**6.4 The flagship experiment and candidate phase gate: the trap-escape test.** Same-seed A/B at the lethal +8 sun: set-point locus live vs. frozen. The literature's pre-registered predictions: (a) escape is threshold-like in mutation supply — at σ 0.03 the collapse (1,200–2,500 ticks) may still outrun selection, and *that is the expected result, not a failure* (§2.4, §4.3); sweep σ within the event-legal range [0, 0.12] to find the escape threshold, if one exists; (b) where escape occurs, the Bogert interaction: selection on the H.5 thermal-compensation locus measurably stalls as behavior re-shields physiology — a two-locus story the per-locus channels can already carry; (c) the standing 8/8 finding is only ever *revised* by measurement, never assumed away. This is to the movement genome what the Yoshida test was to heredity: a specific, falsifiable, chart-visible transformation of a phenomenon we already display — and this time the phenomenon is our own recorded incident. Second experiment, cheaper: rover/sitter invasion-from-rare in both directions (frequency dependence demonstrated, not asserted). Third, free: spatial sorting — after a seeding event, mobility-allele frequency vs. distance from the population core (§2.5), no new mechanism required.

**6.5 Observability is half the phase.** New pure-observer movement metrics (§4.9): MSD regime, occupancy entropy, gradient alignment (per field), mean run length — plus the trap detector, worded "since", calibrated against reference bands measured in the shipped world before any locus goes live. The recorder's two-plane ceiling makes an extension part of this phase's declared scope for any species taking a third locus (Drifta will, if 6.2's #1 and #2 both ship — a decision point for the owner: which two loci Drifta's channels carry, or extend the recorder). Every locus passes the 10-second rail test (§5.4) or is explicitly designated Observatory-only; the elongation grammar ships with, not after, the first mobility locus.

**6.6 Risks, ranked.** R1 **integrator exploits** (Sims/Bibites class): clamp-riding at fast rails, spinner-style scanning, the sessile attractor at slow rails, discretization seams — mitigated by realized-kinematics pricing, the energy-per-distance monotonicity assertion at corridor rails, and field-layout-sampling certification (§4.4); the H.3 corner extinctions say corners are exactly where our seams show. R2 **trap loci trap** — by construction (§4.8); that is a feature to narrate, and the mitigation is the detector, not the reprice. R3 **sign-flip discontinuity**: one-signed loci only (§4.1). R4 **dead knobs**: the 10-second test, and Bibites' warning about threshold/blend parameters. R5 **movement-loci epistasis through position**: joint corridors, not per-locus (§4.6). R6 **drift misread as adaptation** at Venator's N — honesty rule unchanged. R7 **the honesty note**: inherited set-points are the one place this model knowingly departs from biology, which learns them (§3.4) — record it in the plan the way the Yoshida non-reproduction was recorded, before measurement, not after.

The through-line from all four lenses is one sentence: make the *reference signals and gains* of fixed controllers heritable, price the realized motion superlinearly, certify in the loop across field layouts, and instrument the trap — because the movement genome's first honest deliverable is not a rescued world, it is a world that can finally be *asked* whether evolution discovers a better controller than the one we hard-coded and measured killing it.
